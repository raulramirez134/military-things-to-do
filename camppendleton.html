Seasonal Events

Oceanside Sunset Market
A lively weekly street market with food trucks, live music, and local vendors along the pier — a genuine local favorite, not just a tourist stop.
Typical scheduleThursdays, 5–9pm
Oceanside Pier Amphitheater Events
Concerts, fireworks practice sessions, and summer movie nights right on the beach — check the amphitheater's schedule for what's on.
Typical datesVaries, mostly summer
Vista Strawberry Festival
A large regional festival with vendors, live music, and a strawberry run — very popular but crowded; wear good walking shoes and expect a shuttle from parking.
Typical datesLate May
Vista Viking Festival
A fun, family-friendly Nordic-themed festival with archery, axe throwing, live music, and mead vendors — gets hot and crowded midday.
Typical datesVaries, warm-weather months

Dates shift year to year — treat these as a rough seasonal guide and confirm current dates closer to the time. Know of something else worth adding? Use the feedback form below.

Free, confidential, 24/7 — for any service member, veteran, or family member in crisis, including thoughts of self-harm.
NationwideDial 988, then press 1 Chat online →
