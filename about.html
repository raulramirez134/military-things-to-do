About Military Things To Do

Military Things To Do is an independent guide built for service members and their families navigating a new duty station overseas. It started with Ramstein Air Base and the surrounding Kaiserslautern Military Community, mapping out restaurants, grocery stores, language schools, medical and emergency resources, housing, childcare, legal services, and more — all measured against how far they actually are from the gate.

The idea is simple: PCS moves are stressful enough without having to piece together where to eat, where to get a haircut, or who to call in an emergency from scattered forums and outdated Facebook posts. This site tries to put it all in one place, kept current with the help of the community that uses it.

Who runs this

This site is independently built and maintained by a single person with ties to the military community, not by any official government or DoD organization. It is not an official U.S. Department of Defense, U.S. Air Force, or U.S. Army publication, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government.

How it stays accurate

Listings are researched and periodically reviewed, and the community can flag corrections or suggest new listings directly through the site's feedback form and per-listing "Claim / report" links. Visitors can also leave their own reviews on individual listings.

Get in touch

Have a suggestion, correction, or question? Use the feedback form on the main listings page — it goes straight to the person who runs this site.
