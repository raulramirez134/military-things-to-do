Seasonal Events

Week of the Eagles
The 101st Airborne Division (Air Assault)'s annual week of events — a division run, memorial ceremonies, air assault demonstrations, sports competitions and a veterans' reunion. Many events are open to the public; visitors without a DoD ID need a pass from the Gate 7 Visitor Control Center.
Typical datesMid-May (2026: May 15–21)
Montgomery County Fair
A classic county fair at the Clarksville Speedway, 1600 Needmore Rd — carnival rides, a midway, fair food and entertainment. Admission was $8 for adults and $5 for children 4–11 in 2026; 3 and under free.
Typical datesLate May (2026: May 26–30)
Spring into Summer Festival Salutes Fort Campbell
Oak Grove's signature festival honoring Fort Campbell, at the War Memorial Walking Trail Park — concerts, live shows, carnival rides and a drone show. Free entry, parking and rides.
Typical datesLate May (2027: May 29–30)
CMA Fest (Nashville)
Nashville's four-day country music festival — many daytime stages downtown are free, while the nightly concerts at Nissan Stadium need tickets. About an hour's drive; plan parking or a shuttle.
Typical datesEarly June, Thursday–Sunday (2026: June 4–7 · 2027: June 10–13)
Western Kentucky State Fair
A nine-day regional fair at the fairgrounds in Hopkinsville — midway rides, livestock shows, contests and nightly entertainment. Gate admission $3, $1 for military with ID, and free for children 2 and under; rides cost extra.
Typical datesLate June through July 4 (2026: June 26–July 4)
Fort Campbell Independence Day Celebration
The post's Fourth of July celebration on the Division Parade Field — carnival attractions in the afternoon, then a concert and fireworks. Free and open to the public; visitors need a pass from the Gate 7 Visitor Control Center.
Typical datesJuly 4 (2026: July 4)
Let Freedom Sing! Music City July 4th
Nashville's free Independence Day party downtown — several stages of live music and one of the country's biggest fireworks shows over the Cumberland River.
Typical datesAround July 4 (2026: July 3–4)
Oak Grove Butterfly Festival
A free family festival at Oak Grove's War Memorial Walking Trail — a live release of thousands of butterflies, a butterfly house, crafts and kids' shows. Parking is free.
Typical datesEarly September (2027: Sept 4, 11am–4pm)
Tennessee State Fair
Middle Tennessee's big agricultural and family fair at the Nashville Fairgrounds — livestock and farm exhibits, competitions, carnival rides, food and music. Ticket prices vary by day.
Typical datesMid-September, about 10 days (2026: Sept 11–20)
Christmas on the Cumberland
Clarksville's free holiday light walk along the RiverWalk at McGregor Park, 640 N Riverside Dr. Opening day brings a holiday market (2–8pm), photos with Santa, live music and a drone show. The lights are turned off during rain or high wind.
Typical datesNightly from late November through New Year (2026: Nov 21 – Jan 2, 2027)
Clarksville–Montgomery County Lighted Christmas Parade
The city and county's joint holiday parade through downtown Clarksville after dark. Free to watch.
Typical datesFirst Saturday of December, 5–7pm (2026: Dec 5)
Nashville's Big Bash (New Year's Eve)
Nashville's free New Year's Eve concert at Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park, with the Music Note Drop and fireworks at midnight.
Typical datesDecember 31 (2026: Dec 31)

Dates shift year to year and military events can be cancelled or moved on short notice for training and deployments — treat these as a rough seasonal guide and confirm current dates closer to the time. Fort Campbell Police & Fire (non-emergency)
On-post police and fire for anything that isn't an emergency. Off post, use the local department: Clarksville PD (931) 552-1011, Oak Grove PD (270) 439-4602, Hopkinsville PD (270) 890-1300.
Non-emergency(270) 798-7111
Blanchfield Army Community Hospital
The on-post hospital, 650 Joel Dr. The Emergency Center (C Building, 2nd floor) is open 24/7. For a true emergency, dial 911 — don't drive yourself if it can be avoided.
Emergency Center(270) 798-8000 Appointments(270) 798-4677
Poison Control (National)
Suspected poisoning or dangerous exposure — free, 24/7, nationwide.
(800) 222-1222

Support & Well-Being

Veterans / Military Crisis Line
Free, confidential, 24/7 — for any service member, veteran, or family member in crisis, including thoughts of self-harm.
NationwideDial 988, then press 1 Chat online →
Military OneSource
Free 24/7 non-medical counseling and everyday support — relocation, relationships, stress, finances.
800-342-9647 Chat / info online →
Domestic Abuse Victim Advocate
24/7 confidential support for domestic abuse. Family Advocacy Program at BACH: (270) 798-8601 (after hours (270) 798-8400).
(931) 980-5787
SHARP (Sexual Assault Response)
Confidential 24/7 hotline for sexual assault and harassment response. DoD Safe Helpline: 877-995-5247.
(270) 498-4319
On-Call Chaplain
Confidential, faith-neutral support, day or night — after duty hours, call and ask for the on-call chaplain.
(270) 798-9467
American Red Cross (Emergency Messages)
To reach a deployed or TDY soldier about a family emergency, 24/7. Army Emergency Relief financial help goes through ACS: (270) 798-9322.
(877) 272-7337

Numbers current as of publishing — always confirm against Fort Campbell's official Crisis Phone Numbers page, since these do change. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 911 first.
