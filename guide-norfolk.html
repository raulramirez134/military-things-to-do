New Arrival Guide — Naval Station Norfolk

First weeks at Naval Station Norfolk,
step by step.

A general checklist for getting settled — order isn't rigid, and dates depend on your command, but this is roughly how it goes for most sailors and families arriving at the world's largest naval station.

Before you arrive
  • Request a command sponsor as soon as you get orders — your gaining command assigns one through the Navy's Command Sponsorship Program.A good sponsor is genuinely one of the most useful resources you'll have — they know what's actually changed recently and current wait times.
  • Start your household goods paperwork early through the Defense Personal Property System (DPS) — schedule your move as soon as your dates are firm, and decide between a government-coordinated HHG shipment or a Personally Procured Move (PPM/DITY).
  • Consider applying for base housing early through HEAT — the Housing Early Assistance Tool lets you start a housing application before you even arrive.
First week
  • Report to Personnel Support Detachment (PSD) to check in with Personnel, Finance, and your command — Gate 2 off Hampton Blvd is the main entrance, open 24/7 for authorized personnel.
  • Enroll in your command's indoctrination program — Navy policy requires this within 30 days of reporting, and it's genuinely useful for learning how your specific command runs.
  • Enroll in DEERS and update ID cards for every family member at the ID card section on base.
  • Visit the Housing Service Center at Bldg SDA-337, 7924 14th St, if you're considering on-base family housing — it's privatized and managed by Liberty Military Housing across 10 communities organized by rank, and waitlists move slowly, so apply early regardless of whether you plan to live on or off base.
First month
  • Good news on driving: active-duty members stationed in Virginia — plus spouses and dependent children — can legally drive on a valid out-of-state license. You're not required to get a Virginia license unless you want one.If your vehicle is solely owned by you, it can also keep its out-of-state plates.
  • Enroll kids in school as early as possible — Norfolk Public Schools runs 37 elementary, 9 middle, and 5 high schools, and which one you're zoned for depends on exactly where you land, whether that's Norfolk itself or one of the surrounding Hampton Roads cities.See the Schools category on the main listings page for specific options already researched near the base.
  • Register for TRICARE and get set up with a Primary Care Manager — Naval Medical Center Portsmouth is the main Military Treatment Facility, with additional TRICARE Prime Clinics in Virginia Beach and Chesapeake if you land further out.
  • Confirm your BAH is calculating correctly for the Norfolk/Portsmouth Military Housing Area — use ZIP 23511 in the official DTMO BAH calculator to check your exact rate.Virginia doesn't tax military basic pay, which adds real take-home value on top of BAH.
Settling in
  • Find a spouses' club or Family Readiness Group — one of the fastest ways to build a local support network, especially given how often ships and units deploy or rotate out of here.
  • Explore beyond the gate — Naval Station Norfolk sits in the middle of the genuinely large Hampton Roads metro area, so Virginia Beach, Portsmouth, Newport News, and Hampton are all a short drive away once the paperwork phase is behind you.
