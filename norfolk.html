Seasonal Events

Norfolk NATO Festival
The longest continuously running festival in Hampton Roads and the only one in the U.S. honoring NATO — a Parade of Nations with military bands and floats from all NATO member nations, at Town Point Park.
Typical datesApril
Patriotic Festival
A Memorial Day weekend celebration honoring the armed forces at the Norfolk waterfront, with live concert performances at Scope Arena.
Typical datesMemorial Day weekend
Norfolk Harborfest
America's largest, longest-running free maritime festival — a Parade of Sail with tall ships, fireworks, Navy exhibits, and live music along the downtown waterfront.
Typical datesMid-to-late June
Stockley Gardens Arts Festival
A free, well-loved outdoor arts festival in Ghent featuring painting, sculpture, photography, and jewelry from artists across the U.S. and abroad, alongside live music and food.
Typical datesSpring and fall
Virginia International Tattoo
A large-scale military and drum corps performance at Scope Plaza, featuring bagpipers, drummers, and international military bands — timed alongside the NATO Festival.
Typical datesApril

Dates shift year to year and military events can be cancelled on short notice due to deployments — treat these as a rough seasonal guide and confirm current dates closer to the time. Know of something else worth adding? Use the feedback form below.
