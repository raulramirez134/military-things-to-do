Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Military Things To Do ("we," "us," or "this site") is an independent, privately-run resource for military personnel and families. This policy explains what information is collected when you use this site and how it's used.

Information we collect

We collect limited information automatically and through features you choose to use:

  • Reviews: If you submit a review, we store the name you provide (or "Anonymous" if left blank), your rating, your written review, and the date submitted. Do not include personal contact information in a review.
  • Feedback form submissions: Messages sent through the feedback form are delivered to the site owner's email and are not published publicly.
  • Usage data: Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) automatically logs standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.
  • Local browser storage: Your saved favorites are stored only in your own browser (not on our servers) and are not shared with anyone.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display advertisements served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad personalization settings at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, UK, or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent regarding cookies and personalized advertising before ads are shown, in accordance with applicable law.

How we use information

We use the information above to operate and improve the site, respond to feedback, moderate reviews, and display advertising. We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

This site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. Google Fonts and Google AdSense are used for typography and advertising respectively. Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing data they process.

Data retention and removal

Reviews remain published until removed by site administration. If you'd like a review you submitted removed, or have any other privacy question, use the feedback form on the main listings page.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the feedback form on the main listings page.

Military Things To Do is an independent community resource, not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Defense.
